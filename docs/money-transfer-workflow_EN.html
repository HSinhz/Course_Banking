💠 Core banking — the non-negotiables when moving money

The core banking system is the single source of truth for money; mobile / ATM / internet are just channels. Whatever the channel, a money transfer must obey these principles — the protection rules (P#) below are how this demo enforces them. Click a chip to see the rule.

⚛️
1. Atomic — all or nothing

Debit, credit and the record commit together in one transaction, or not at all. Never a “debited but not credited” half transfer.

P2P8
🔁
2. Idempotent — exactly once

One logical transfer takes effect once, no matter how many times it is sent (retry, double-click, two parallel requests).

P1P3P4
⚖️
3. Conserved — double-entry

Every movement is a balanced Debit = Credit pair; total money in the system never changes (only shifts between accounts).

P8P9P11
🔒
4. Concurrency-safe — isolation

Two operations on the same account at the same moment can’t overdraw it or lose an update (no “lost update”).

P5P6
🎯
5. Exact money

Amounts are decimal, never float — no rounding drift (0.1 + 0.2 must equal 0.3).

P7
💾
6. Durable — crash-safe

Once accepted, a transfer survives process crashes; the “next step” instruction is never lost and gets finished.

P13P14
📜
7. Auditable — immutable ledger

An append-only ledger (never edited/deleted) lets you reconstruct any balance and reconcile every transaction.

P8P9P16
🚦
8. Definite state — never guess

Every transfer has a defined state (Pending → Completed / Reversed). On uncertainty (timeout) it holds and is reconciled — it never assumes.

P15P17P18
↩️
9. Converges — sent or refunded

Money always ends fully sent or fully refunded — never stuck half-way. In-flight funds sit safely in a suspense account.

P10P12
🛡️
10. Authorized — right actor only

Only the authenticated owner — ideally with a second factor (OTP) — can move money; internal accounts are never exposed.
